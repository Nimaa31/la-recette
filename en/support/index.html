Help

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Answers to the most common questions.

Delete my data

Open Profile, then tap “Delete my data”. Everything the app saved on your phone is erased: first name, answers and analyses.

Cancel my subscription

The subscription is managed by Apple, not in the app. On your iPhone: Settings, your name at the top, Subscriptions, Décrypte, then Cancel Subscription.

Get Premium back on a new iPhone

Open the Premium screen, or Profile, and tap “Restore purchases”. You need to be signed in with the same Apple account.

The app says my screenshot is unreadable

Pick screenshots of a text conversation, uncropped, with readable text and the name visible at the top of the screen. A photo or another kind of screen cannot be analyzed.

Export a WhatsApp chat

  1. In WhatsApp, open the chat and tap the name at the top
  2. Tap Export chat, then Without media
  3. Choose Save to Files, then come back here

Are my screenshots kept?

No. Our server does not keep them, and the app only saves the result as text. The details are in the privacy policy.
