Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What the app knows about you

Your first name and your answers stay on your phone. Décrypte has no account: no email and no phone number is collected.

Your screenshots

When you start an analysis, your screenshots are sent encrypted to our server, which passes them to Anthropic to read the conversation. Our server does not keep them. Anthropic may keep them for a limited time to prevent abuse, without using them to train its models.

WhatsApp export

When you import a WhatsApp export, it is read and analyzed on your phone. No message is sent or kept: only the result (statistics and verdict) is saved.

Other people’s messages

Your screenshots contain someone else’s messages. Only import conversations you are part of, and do not share the result in a way that could harm that person.

What stays on your phone

The result of each analysis, text only, is saved on your phone. You can erase everything at any time from Profile, then Delete my data.

Our providers

Supabase (server), Anthropic (AI analysis), RevenueCat and Apple (subscription).

Contact us

For any question about your data, use the Help page.
